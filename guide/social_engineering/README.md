# Social Engineering

Social engineering leverages human psychology to gain unauthorized access or obtain sensitive information. This section covers fundamental and advanced techniques used by attackers, along with awareness and mitigation strategies.

## Beginner
- Understand the principles of social engineering and common attack types (phishing, pretexting, baiting).
- Learn to recognize and craft basic phishing emails and messages.
- Study legal and ethical considerations around social engineering and red-team engagements.

## Intermediate
- Develop advanced pretexts and scripts for voice and in-person engagements (vishing, physical social engineering).
- Conduct reconnaissance to gather information that increases the success of social engineering attacks.
- Use tools like the Social-Engineer Toolkit (SET) to automate phishing campaigns and payload delivery.

## Advanced
- Plan and execute multi-channel social engineering campaigns that combine email, phone, and physical tactics.
- Analyze psychological triggers and persuasion techniques (e.g., authority, urgency, reciprocity) to enhance engagements.
